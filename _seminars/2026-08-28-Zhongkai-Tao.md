abstract: >
    It is expected that for a random closed hyperbolic surface, the Laplace eigenfunctions are delocalized and equidistributed. I will explain how to use the recently developed polynomial method to prove a polynomially improved L^\infty bound for the Laplace eigenfunctions on the random covers of a hyperbolic surface. This is joint work with Elena Kim.
