abstract: >
  Motivated by the construction of optimal sample sets for numerical integration, Zaremba (1971) proposed the following celebrated conjecture in number theory: there exists an absolute constant M > 0 such that every natural number q is the denominator of some reduced fraction a/q whose partial quotients are bounded by M. That is, the continued fraction expansion
  a/q = 1 / (a1 + 1 / (a2 + 1 / (... + 1 / an)))
  satisfies ai <= M for all 1 <= i <= n. In this talk, I will survey the history of Zaremba's conjecture and outline the methods behind its recent resolution.
