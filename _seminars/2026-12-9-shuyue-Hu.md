abstract: |
AI agents are increasingly capable of learning, interacting, and reasoning in complex environments, opening up new opportunities for both understanding collective intelligence and advancing scientific research. In this talk, I will present our research along two directions. First, I will discuss multi-agent learning dynamics, focusing on how agents adapt through repeated interactions and how their individual learning behaviors give rise to complex collective dynamics. Second, I will introduce our recent work on agentic formal verification, which aims to transform AI-generated mathematical proofs into trustworthy, machine-verifiable knowledge. In particular, I will present Turing Agent, a framework that orchestrates theorem formalization and proof verification to tackle research-level mathematical problems.
