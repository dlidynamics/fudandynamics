abstract: |
  Sullivan's no-wandering-domain theorem asserts that, for every rational map of degree at least two in one complex variable, each Fatou component is eventually periodic. In this talk, I will present a new proof based on hyperbolic area, without using quasiconformal techniques.

  I will then discuss further developments of this method. In particular, we resolve a long-standing open question dating back more than 40 years, proving that transcendental entire and meromorphic functions have no bounded-orbit wandering domains. (This is joint work with Kostiantyn Drach, Leticia Pardo-Simón, Nikolai Prochorov, Lasse Rempe, Beno Učakar, and James Waterman.)

  Finally, I will discuss an extension of the method to higher-dimensional holomorphic dynamics. We establish the absence of bounded-orbit wandering domains for elliptic skew products. As a consequence, we completely resolve the local no-wandering-domain problem for polynomial skew products near elliptic invariant fibers.
