abstract: >
Pick invertible $d \times d$ matrices at random, independently, according to some fixed probability distribution $\nu$,  and multiply them successively. The norm of the product evolves at a well-defined (deterministic) exponential rate, the largest Lyapunov exponent $\lambda_+$. We prove that $\lambda_+$ is a continuous function of $\nu$. This is a joint work with C. Bocker when $d=2$, and with A. Avila and A. Eskin in the general case.
